Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does carpet installation cost in Melbourne?

The price depends on the carpet you choose, the underlay, the size and shape of the rooms and whether there are stairs. We give free, no-obligation quotes with a clear price before any work starts — call 0415 850 095 or send us a quote request.

Do you supply the carpet as well as install it?

Yes. We supply and install, so you deal with one team from choosing your carpet through to the final clean-up.

How long does it take to carpet a house?

Most single rooms take a few hours. A typical house is usually finished in one to two days, depending on size and stairs.

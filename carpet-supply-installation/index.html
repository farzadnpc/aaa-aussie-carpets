Carpet that suits the way you live

Every home is different. A busy family room with kids and pets needs something tougher than a guest bedroom, and a rental property needs something different again. We'll talk you through the options and bring samples so you can see and feel them in your own light.

  • Wool — natural, soft underfoot and long-lasting
  • Nylon & solution-dyed nylon — hard-wearing and stain resistant
  • Polyester & blends — great value with a soft feel
  • Loop pile, cut pile, twist & plush styles in a wide range of colours

What's included in a carpet installation

  • Measure and free, no-obligation quote
  • Uplift and removal of old carpet and underlay
  • New underlay matched to your carpet and traffic
  • Power-stretched installation so the carpet stays flat
  • Neat seams, trims and transitions to tiles or timber
  • Clean-up when we're done

Why the installation matters

Even the best carpet will ripple, fray or show seams if it's laid poorly. Our installers stretch every room properly, plan seams away from traffic and light, and finish doorways and edges so they look sharp and last. Tough floors, built to last.

Common questions

How long does carpet installation take?

Most single rooms are done in a few hours, and a typical house is usually finished in one to two days. We'll give you a time frame with your quote.

Do you remove the old carpet?

Yes. We can uplift and take away your old carpet and underlay as part of the job — just let us know when you ask for a quote.

Do I need new underlay?

In most cases, yes. Old underlay is often flattened or worn, and fresh underlay makes new carpet feel better and last longer. We'll advise you on site.
